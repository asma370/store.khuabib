Khubaib
Atelier & Streetwear Architecture

Khubaib was founded as an antidote to disposable fast fashion. Rooted in brutalist subculture, gothic typography, and oversized Japanese silhouette engineering, each piece is designed to be worn heavily and aged with character.

Every garment on our signature rail is made from deadstock or organic heavy-gauge cottons (up to 480 GSM) with hand-treated washes and custom metal alloy hardware. We maintain a strict zero-restock policy.
